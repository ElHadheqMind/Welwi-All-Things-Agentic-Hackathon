# Welwi — Demo Narration Script

Millions of visually impaired people already have AI that lets them see. Point a phone at a room, and it'll describe the room. Point it at a sign, and it'll read the sign. That part's been solved. But seeing isn't the same as living — none of those tools remember your day, keep your notes, manage your calendar, or actually do anything with what they just saw. There's no manager. So we built one: a multi-agent app that doesn't just describe your world, it runs your day alongside you.

This is Welwi. No login. No home screen. No app to figure out. You open it, and it's already listening — a voice, already there, waiting to talk.

And here's what makes this different from every other assistant you've seen. We are not building an LLM that just tells you what's in front of you. Plenty of tools can describe a room, read a sign, narrate a scene, and then stop — leaving you to do something about it yourself. That's not what this is. We built an agent that guides you through the action itself — takes the words, does the work, and confirms it's done. Watch.

"What's on my calendar today?" And without a single tap, Welwi reads back the whole day — morning routine, breakfast, the walk, lunch, the doctor's visit this afternoon, all of it, spoken back in seconds, pulled live from a real calendar, not a canned demo list.

"Actually, can you move my doctor's visit?" And it just... does. No settings screen, no date picker, no scrolling through a calendar grid with your thumb. You say the change, Welwi confirms it back to you in one sentence, and it's rescheduled — live, on the same real event you just heard listed a moment ago.

"What's on my grocery list? What should I buy?" Welwi reads it back — the actual note, saved from an earlier conversation, not a fake prop pulled up for the camera. "Add apples to it." One sentence, and the list updates itself, right then, in the middle of a conversation that was never about lists in the first place.

Then you hold up your phone to a poster on the wall — a DevFest event flyer. "Add this to my calendar." Welwi's camera opens on its own, reads the flyer in real time, and proposes the event back to you: the name, the date, the time. You confirm, and it's on your calendar — an event that started as ink on a wall five seconds ago. Not a description of the poster. An action taken because of it.

And here's the proof, no smoke and mirrors: switch over to the normal screen — the one a sighted friend or a judge would actually look at — and there it is. Apples, sitting in the grocery note, exactly where the conversation put it. The DevFest event, sitting on the calendar, exactly where the camera saw it. Nothing staged after the fact. Everything you just heard happen, actually happened.

And then, without anyone asking anything — "Oh, quick heads up, it's almost time for your doctor's visit." Nobody touched the phone. Nobody opened it. As you can see, our multi-agent system doesn't just wait for you to ask — it reminds you, out loud, on its own. That's Welwi.

Under all of it: Gemini, live, reasoning turn by turn, orchestrated through Google's own Agent Development Kit, running on Cloud Run, backed by real Firestore data — one continuous agent, not a script pretending to be one.

Welwi isn't an app you operate, and it isn't a narrator describing your life back to you. It's the manager that was always missing — already listening, already watching, already doing, before you even finish the sentence.
